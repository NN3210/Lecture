# 画像生成記録

2026年10月14日。組み込み画像生成ツールを使用。外部の実測写真や実験データではなく、教育用の概念図。

## 測距の導入イラスト（survey_hero.png）

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: Japanese university surveying lecture illustration, landscape 16:9. Create a beautiful refined editorial illustration of a total station on a tripod at left observing a survey prism pole at right across a quiet grassy field, pale warm ivory background, muted navy and teal equipment, subtle ochre sunlight, fine precise details, generous whitespace upper half. No people, no brands, no logos, no letters, no numbers, no formulas. The instrument and target must be realistic and grounded with three tripod legs. A faint straight sightline links instrument to target, clear distant hills, subtle atmospheric texture. This is an introductory conceptual illustration, not a technical data chart. Restrained sophisticated scientific textbook style, luminous and clear, no cartoon faces, no clutter.
```

## 観測値と組平均の概念図（mean_targets.png）

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Create one landscape 16:9 conceptual illustration for a Japanese university lecture about repeated measurements and averages. Beautiful precise minimalist scientific editorial artwork on pure warm ivory #FAF8F2 background. TWO equally sized circular survey targets side by side, seen perfectly front-on, with delicate thin navy concentric rings and a small ochre cross at their identical centers. Left target: 20 tiny teal dots broadly dispersed on both sides of center, roughly symmetric, some near inner rings and others near outer rings. Right target: 20 tiny ochre dots clustered tightly around the center, with a slight symmetric spread, not exactly at the center. The left illustrates individual readings, the right illustrates averages of groups, but add NO TEXT, NO SYMBOLS, NO FORMULAS, NO LABELS, NO NUMERICAL SCALE. Empty whitespace around the two targets for slide text outside this image. Absolutely equal target size and ring spacing for fair comparison. Sophisticated mathematical book illustration, clear crisp edges, subtle paper texture, no gradients, no arrows, no unrelated objects. This is a conceptual illustration not an actual experimental dataset.
```

生成後、機器・標的・配色・図の関係を目視確認した。標的図の点は数値データではなく、比率や標準偏差を読み取る用途には使わない。スライド上にも概念図であることを明記する。
